Admissions

Join a learning environment built around practical skills and opportunity.

The admissions process is straightforward, transparent and designed to help applicants move from enquiry to enrolment without unnecessary confusion.

Entry Requirements

  • KCPE certificate or result slip
  • KCSE certificate or result slip
  • National ID card or copy
  • Birth certificate
  • Admission letter or placement confirmation where applicable

Application Windows

Applications are typically processed in the January and September intake cycles.

Recommended closing dates: 31 January and 30 September each year.

How to Apply

  1. Complete the online application form through the official college website.
  2. Upload the required academic and identity documents.
  3. Pay the application and admission fee through the official online payment channel on the website.
  4. Await review and confirmation from the admissions office.
  5. Complete registration and reporting instructions once admission is confirmed.

Fees & Payments

All fees, including admission and registration charges, are paid through the College’s official online payment system on the website. This ensures secure processing and a clear record for each applicant.

Indicative fees: Admission fee Kshs. 500 and KUCCPS placement fee Kshs. 1,500 as applicable.

Important: Students should only use the official payment channels listed on the website and portal.
