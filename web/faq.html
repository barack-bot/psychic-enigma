Student Support

Frequently asked questions from applicants and students.

We have kept this section practical and direct so parents and learners can find answers quickly before they contact the office.

How do I pay my fees?

All payments are made through the official online payment channel on the college website and student portal. Applicants should complete payment only through the official instructions provided by the institution.

What is the process for applying to the college?

Applicants complete the online form, upload the required documents, pay the required fee through the website and await confirmation from the admissions office. Thereafter, the registration process is shared by the academic office.

What if I forget my portal PIN or password?

Students should use the password reset function in the student portal. If the problem persists, they should contact the college support desk for identity verification and reissue.

When are the major intakes?

The main intake periods are January and September. Exact application deadlines are published on the website and student portal each year.

What if I miss a term or need to defer my studies?

Students are encouraged to inform the college as early as possible. Deferral and missed-term arrangements are handled according to academic policy and institutional guidance.

Are the programmes TVET and industry focused?

Yes. The college offers programmes designed to prepare learners for employment, the informal sector, enterprise and further technical study. Many courses are aligned to practical skills demanded by the local economy.
