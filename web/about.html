About the College

Preparing learners for work, enterprise and lifelong growth.

Kitutu Chache Technical and Vocational College is committed to practical, market-responsive training that equips students to thrive in employment, self-employment and community development.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in research, skills development and innovation that responds to the evolving needs of industry and the community.

Our Mission

To promote technical and vocational education, applied research, science, technology and innovation in ways that empower learners, families and local enterprises.

Core Values

  • Relevance
  • Excellence
  • Integrity
  • Equity
  • Quality

Campus Life

Facilities built for practical learning.

Students learn by doing, supported by workshops, labs and student services that reflect real workplace conditions.

Workshops

Hands-on practice in masonry, plumbing, fabrication and mechanical trades using training equipment aligned to industry standards.

Engineering Labs

Purpose-built practical spaces for solar installation, electrical work, electronics and technical troubleshooting.

Creative & Service Units

Fashion, hospitality and beauty spaces where learners develop professional competence and customer-facing confidence.

Library & Learning Support

Study resources, academic guidance and digital support to reinforce technical training and personal development.

Sports & Co-curricular

Student life beyond the classroom promotes leadership, teamwork and healthier routines.

Boarding & Residence

Supportive residential accommodation for students from across the region who need a safe and focused study environment.

Leadership

Guided by people who understand skills, discipline and service.

Our leadership team supports quality learning, student wellbeing and institutional accountability.

Principal’s Message

At Kitutu Chache TVC, we are committed to producing confident, competent graduates who can contribute productively to the economy and their communities.

Our approach combines technical competence with discipline, professionalism and practical exposure, so that every learner is prepared for the realities of work.
